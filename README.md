# CruwUP

**Share rides with people from your campus or workplace.**

CruwUP is a carpooling app for university students and office teams in Karachi. Offer a seat in your car, find a ride that matches your route, chat with your driver or passengers, and get to class or work for less.

## Features

- **Rides for your community.** Join your campus or workplace and see rides offered by people in it.
- **Offer or request a seat.** Drivers post rides with their route and stops. Passengers book a seat for their pickup and drop-off.
- **Multi-ride journeys.** If no single ride covers your whole trip, CruwUP can chain rides together.
- **Live map.** See routes and stops on a map and share your location in chat.
- **Chat.** Message your driver or passengers about a ride, send private messages, and talk in your campus or workplace group.
- **Notifications.** Get alerts for new requests, confirmations, cancellations and ride reminders, even when the app is closed.
- **Ratings.** Rate drivers and passengers after a trip to keep the community trustworthy.

## How to install

1. **Tap the download button above** on your Android phone. The file `CruwUP.apk` downloads straight away.
2. **Open the file** from your browser's download notification or your Downloads folder.
3. If Android asks, tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source** (also called *Install unknown apps*) for your browser or file manager, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**.
5. If **Play Protect** shows a warning, tap **More details** and then **Install anyway**. This message appears for any app installed outside the Play Store.
6. Open **CruwUP**, create an account, verify your email, and choose your campus or workplace.

> You can turn "Install unknown apps" off again once the installation finishes.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| The download doesn't start | Open the link in Chrome, or copy the direct link into your browser's address bar. |
| "App not installed" | Uninstall any older version of CruwUP, then install again. Check that you have free storage space. |
| "Blocked by Play Protect" | Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. |
| "Install blocked" | Enable **Install unknown apps** for your browser in Settings, then open the file again. |
| The app is slow on first open | The server wakes up after being idle. Wait about a minute and try again. |
| No verification email | Check your spam folder, then request a new code. |
| No notifications | Allow notifications for CruwUP in Android settings, and turn off battery optimisation for the app. |
